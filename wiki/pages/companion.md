# Your companion

Your companion is the little character at the foot of the menu. Now and then it wanders along the edges of the page, and it pauses when you come near. It talks with you, keeps one list of what needs you, greets you when you come back, and meets your friends' companions. It's called Mefi until you give it a name.

## Open the companion menu

Click your companion, or press **Esc** when nothing else is open. A ring of six bubbles opens over what you were doing. The center, **Return to studio**, closes it. **Esc** steps back out of a bubble, then closes the menu. Opening the menu never changes how your agents run.

| Bubble | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Talk** | Chat, plan or make a task. **What are you doing?**, **What's next?** and **Recap today** are one tap away. |
| **What I'm doing** | What's being built, the team and what they told each other, and recent activity. |
| **Needs you** | Everything waiting on you, with the actions right there. |
| **Suggest work** | Send an idea to the inbox as your own request, or take one of its picks with **Work on it**. Nothing starts by itself. |
| **Friends** | Opens Friends: The Lobby, Rooms, the Playground and Your PCs. |
| **Personality** | How it behaves, the run controls and its settings. |

**Audio link**, at the bottom of the menu, opens Music & video.

## Talk

Ask about your project, think an idea through, or ask your companion to create a task. Replies use the name you gave it and know which screen you're on. You need an AI connected first. See [Connect an AI](#/connections).

Your companion talks through the **Companion** seat. To change its model, open **Team › Seats and models**.

<span class="status next">New in 0.5</span> Talk and Suggest work open with the cursor in their box, and the letters you type go there instead of setting off shortcuts.

## Needs you

One list holds everything that waits on you, such as questions and decisions, with their actions in place. It's the same list as the Inbox (`Ctrl J`), and its count shows on your companion, in the top bar and on the tray icon.

- **Clear list** takes stuck items off the list. It asks once more first. Open asks close as left for review, and their cards stay on the board.
- An ask you answer leaves the list at once. Asks older than two days, and asks whose card left the board, close when the project loads.

Which asks Studio may settle for you depends on your permission mode. See [Permissions and decisions](#/permissions).

## Welcome back

After ten minutes or more away, or when your PC wakes, your companion greets you with what finished, what stopped and what needs you. It also reports work done in the project folder while Studio was closed.

## Make it yours

Set its name in **Settings › General › Profile & startup › Companion name**. Chat replies and the menu use it. To keep it still, turn off **Let your companion move** in **Settings › Appearance**. Motion set to **Calm** or **Off** keeps it still too.

Everything else is in the **Personality** bubble:

- **Companion look**: **Wisp**, **Fox**, **Owl**, **Cat** or **Person**.
- **Roam around the studio** lets it wander along clear screen edges. You can also drag it anywhere, and **Pin companion position** keeps it there.
- **Show speech bubbles** turns its little speech bubbles on or off.
- **Show project growth** shows three small lights for what it has learned: project knowledge, workflows that worked, and your preferences. They never change what it may do or answer for you.
- **Project reach** covers the **Current project** or **All projects**.
- The run controls you know from elsewhere in Studio: **Allow new work**, **Run the queue** and **Proactive suggestions**.

## Personality and petting <span class="status next">New in 0.5</span>

Under **Personality**, pick how your companion behaves:

- **Straight work**: short, plain answers. No small talk, faces or wandering.
- **Balanced** (the default): warm and to the point. It reacts when things happen.
- **Friendly & expressive**: chatty and playful, with little faces, idle play and celebrations.

Personality changes faces, idle play, roaming and how replies sound. It never changes what your companion may do. You can still flip each switch yourself: **Little faces and reactions**, **Idle play while you work** and **Roam around the studio**.

To pet your companion, stroke it back and forth with the pointer. It leans in and hearts rise. The bottom of the companion menu keeps your bond, such as “Together 12 days · 40 pets · 3 playdates”. With idle play on, it does something small now and then, and dozes after ten quiet minutes until you touch it.

## Friends and playdates

The **Friends** bubble opens Friends, a place of its own, at the right page: **The Lobby**, **Rooms**, the **Playground**, where your companion meets friends' companions, and [Your PCs](#/your-pcs). **Practice with Pip** works on your own PC with nothing sent.

Rooms and playdates with friends run on the Mefi Studio relay, which is built into Studio, after you sign in with Discord once. [Friends, rooms and playdates](#/friends-and-rooms) covers all of it.
