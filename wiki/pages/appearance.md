# Themes, node styles and looks

Make Studio yours: a color theme, a style for the live node tree, a layout, and as much glass and motion as you like. Every look is free, including the Void collection. You don't need Discord or an account for any of them.

## Open Appearance

Press **U**, or open **Settings › Appearance**. The sidebar sits beside the live tree, so you see each change as you make it. It has four sections: **Theme**, **Nodes**, **Layout** and **Interface**.

- **2D**, **3D** and **Fit** change the preview of the tree.
- The arrow button moves the sidebar to the other side of the window.
- **Esc** or the close button closes Appearance.

## Color themes

Pick one of seven themes: **Aurora** (the default), **Studio gold**, **Midnight**, **Forest**, **Violet**, **Ember** and **Rose**.

The **Void collection** adds four two-tone themes, each with a second color of its own: **Void**, **Eclipse**, **Abyss** and **Neon Dusk**. They sit under their own heading, and since 0.4.4 they're free for everyone, like every other look.

Choose **Custom palette** to pick your own **Accent**, **Background**, **Panels** and **Text** colors, with the color picker or a `#RRGGBB` value. Studio adjusts text and borders so they stay readable. **Reset custom colors** starts over.

Colors are separate from node style and layout, so you can mix any of them.

## Node styles

The **Nodes** section changes how each agent and task looks on the live tree. Each style has its own shape, motion, wires and finishing effect.

| Style | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic orbs** | Luminous circles |
| **Soft glass** | Translucent surfaces |
| **Minimal** | Quiet points |
| **Halo** | Luminous rings |
| **Crystal** | Faceted gems |
| **Singularity** (Void collection) | A black hole with a turning disc |
| **Prism** (Void collection) | A turning crystal that splits light |
| **Sigil** (Void collection) | Hex runes that assemble as it works |

Two **Effects** add to any style: **Blue orbit trails** circle queued and running work, and **Extra glow** brightens halos and cores. Reduced motion pauses the orbit trails.

**Node tree details**, in the same section, holds:

- **Backdrop**, the sky behind the tree. **Follow theme** matches your theme, or pick Aurora ribbons, Deep space, Nebula, Rising embers, Fireflies, Soft bokeh, Warm dust, Quiet grid or Minimal.
- **Speech bubbles**, where agents say what they're doing beside their orb.
- **Card style**: **Auto**, **Outline** or **Filled**.
- **Zen mode**: after 30 seconds without input, the panels fade and the camera tours the tree. Move, click or type to come back.

## Layouts

Under **Arrange the tree**, choose **Constellation** (an open arrangement), **Branches** (a clear hierarchy), **Rings** (concentric groups), **Helix** (a rising spiral) or **Terraces** (stacked levels). Existing nodes keep their places as work updates.

The **Layout** section also holds two sets of controls that you'll find in Music & video too:

- **Tree modes & movement**: what moves the tree (**Steady**, **Music**, **Video** or **Music + video**), and a **Live shape**, either your chosen layout or a **Ring**, **Wave** or **Spiral**, with its width, height, rotation, position and node size.
- **Tree brightness & outlines**: **Node brightness** and **Connecting line brightness** from 0% to 200%, and **Node outlines** for a contrasting edge around each node.

[Music, video and the player](#/media-player/let-the-tree-move-with-the-music) explains how music and video move the tree.

## Interface: glass, glow and motion

**Studio styling** sets the feel of every page. Pick one, then fine-tune it:

- **Focus**: a quiet tint and precise edges.
- **Studio**: soft glass and balanced depth.
- **Atmosphere**: open glass and richer colour.

Then adjust **Density** (**Comfortable** or **Compact**), **Glass intensity** and **Glow intensity**. With glass at zero, reading surfaces turn solid.

- **Motion**: **Full** plays every animation, **Calm** stops looping motion, and **Off** also stops node motion and transitions. Studio follows the reduced-motion setting in Windows too.
- **Blur behind panels**: turn it off to give sheets, menus and Home's panels a plain, darker backdrop.
- **Let your companion move**: turn it off to keep your [companion](#/companion) still.

<span class="status next">Coming in 0.5</span> **Interface scale** makes the whole interface 70% to 150% of its size, text included, and keeps it for the next launch. Find it in **Configuration** under **UI & Surfaces**. Open Configuration with **Ctrl + Shift + ,** or **All settings in one place** in Settings.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **U** | Open Appearance |
| **V** | Switch between the 3D orbit and a flat 2D map |
| **L** | Cycle node labels: auto, updates, all or none |
| **C** | Cycle the camera: overview, follow or free |
| **Space** | Pause or resume the spin |

**V**, **L**, **C** and **Space** work in [Command view](#/command-center). [Navigation and shortcuts](#/shortcuts) lists the rest.
