# Command view and the node tree

Command view shows your project's tasks, sessions and agents in one moving tree. Press `D` or open **Agents › Live › Command**.

Use it to see what is running, follow a task and answer questions without leaving the work.

![Command view with the live tree and work panel](../../assets/shots/command.webp)

## Find the work you care about

Use the search box to find a task or session. Click its node or label to open the details on the right.

The assistant sits near the center. Agents move toward the work they are doing, and their speech bubbles describe their current activity. Task labels show status and progress. Finished work stays available in the task's details even when the tree folds it into a larger group.

The tree is an overview. Open the task's evidence to review what changed and which checks passed.

## Use the side panel

| Tab | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Work** | Running work, queued tasks and items that need attention. |
| **Assistant** | Your conversation with the assistant. |
| **Runs** | Recent build attempts, grouped by task. |
| **Ask** | Questions waiting for your answer. |

Selecting a node opens its details in the same panel. Press `Esc` to step back out of the focused view. In a narrow window, details appear in a floating card.

**Agents** in the top toolbar opens queue settings, including Autopilot, Parallel builds and Build mode. These controls are covered in [The assistant and your agents](assistant.md).

## Move around

- **Overview** keeps the tree framed; **Follow** tracks active work.
- **Fit** or `F` rearranges the tree and brings it into view.
- `Space` pauses or resumes the tree's spin. It does not pause coding agents.
- **View** contains 2D/3D, zoom and label controls.
- **Legend** explains the symbols. **Usage** opens the usage summary.

If you want the tree to stay still, pause the spin and choose a view that is easy to read.

## Make it feel like your space

Open **Settings › Appearance** with `U`. Choose a theme, node style, layout and backdrop, then adjust the effects in the live preview.

All themes and node styles are free in 0.4.4, including the Void collection. You do not need to join or link Discord to keep a style.

## Style & sound

Open **Music & video** for local tracks, radio and supported media links. Closing the player panel keeps playback running.

Audio reactions can make the tree respond to a track, desktop audio or microphone input. Choose the input and connect it from the audio controls. Microphone input here drives visual effects; it is separate from the planned voice assistant.

For reviewing completed work, continue to [Verification and storage](verification.md).
