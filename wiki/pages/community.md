# Media and community

You can play music or video inside Studio while agents work. To meet other people using it, join the [Void Engine Discord](https://discord.gg/xgfKc5pVxG).

## Play something while you work

Open the media player and paste a supported video or audio link. You can also search YouTube in the built-in explorer and add results to your queue.

- Keep the player floating above your work, or use the video as the background of Command view.
- Add links to the queue to play them in order.
- Use **Browse here** to open a website in the player.
- Play local music or radio, and enable the audio link if you want the node tree to react to sound.

These personal media features are available in 0.4.4. They don't require joining Discord.

## Join the Discord

Share a project, ask for feedback, compare setups or keep someone company while you work. You don't need a finished app or a Studio download to join.

The server also has a new bot called **Studio**. Try it in the server and let us know what you'd like it to help with. The bot and the desktop app are separate tools.

## Watch and listen together

Shared playback is still rolling out. It needs a configured rooms hub and a linked Discord account, so the controls may not be available in your build or setup.

When connected:

1. Choose a room in **Listen together**.
2. The host plays a link for the room and controls playback.
3. Other members choose **Listen along** to follow the host's playback position.

**Share what I'm playing** is a separate, optional setting. It lets the connected Discord bot report your current media through `/nowplaying`.

All themes and node styles are free in 0.4.4. Linking Discord isn't required to use the Void collection.

## Ideas we're exploring

We want to bring community conversations and cowork spaces into Studio, so people and their agents can work on projects together. These aren't included in 0.4.4.

“Hey Studio” is another proposed feature: an optional wake phrase that opens a listening popup. The intended flow is to transcribe with a service you choose, let you edit the text, then send it to your existing chat or agent connection. Spoken replies would be optional too. No voice service is selected or installed by the current app.

Tell us which of these would help you, or suggest a different direction, in [Discord](https://discord.gg/xgfKc5pVxG) or a [feature request](https://github.com/nateecho32-stack/mefi-studio/issues/new?template=feature_request.md).
