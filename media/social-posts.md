## Discord

**Mefi's Studio AI+ has grown a brain.**

From circles bumping into chaos to a tree of connected work. Here’s a 30-second development preview, built around what’s changed since 0.3.0:

- **Agent Brain:** see the pipeline, its steps and the agents working on them.
- **Project map:** explore your systems, their parts and the files inside.
- **Playbook:** reuse pipeline patterns from previous work.
- **Plans:** write with an AI partner that explores project files and offers editable suggestions.
- **A cleaner workspace:** clearer navigation, current activity and task history.
- **More model choices:** OpenRouter alongside your existing providers and CLI logins.
- **Stay in the flow:** music, radio, links and a floating video player.
- **A steadier loop:** fewer repeat questions, clearer review states and recorded checks.

Free, open source and local-first. Bring your own provider or local model.
