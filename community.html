Hang out in the Void Engine Discord

Void Engine is Studio's community server. People share what they're building, swap model setups and keep each other company while they work. First-timers and unfinished projects are welcome, and you don't need Studio to join.

Join the Discord ↗

What people do there

Show what you're making

/showcase posts your project to #showcase with a short blurb and tags. You can thank someone who helped, and nobody gets pinged.

Cowork together

/cowork start with a topic opens a temporary voice channel and a thread. Both go away 5 minutes after the voice channel empties.

Get unstuck

Ask in a help thread. When a reply solves it, Mark as solution thanks the helper. /ask answers questions from the Studio guide.

Hear about new builds

Each new Studio release is posted once in the server, with a few highlights and the download link.

Studio, the community bot

The server's bot helps with sharing, coworking and questions. It runs on the owner's own PC, so it's online only while that PC runs it, and now and then it may be away.

In the Discord while the bot is online

  • /showcase Post a project with a title, a link and a description.
  • /cowork start A temporary voice channel and thread to work side by side.
  • /ask Answers from the Studio guide, up to 5 a day each.
  • Mark as solution The person who asked thanks whoever solved it.
  • /me Your points, streak and roles, shown only to you.
  • /privacy and /forget-me See what the bot keeps, or delete your records. It never stores message text.
  • Release posts One post per new Studio release.

Rolling out needs the rooms hub online

  • /room list and /room request Find rooms and ask to join. The room owner says yes.
  • Listen together One shared player per room.
  • /nowplaying What a member's Studio is playing, only if they turned sharing on.

Roles for taking part

Roles come from showing up, sharing and helping, never from message counts or from inviting people. Regular takes four active weeks in a row. Builder, Helper and Mentor need a moderator's OK. Spotlight is the project of the week, for 7 days. Cowork Host, Room Host, Patron and Contributor are given by moderators.

/me shows your points and streak to you alone. There's no public leaderboard.

House rules

  1. Be kind. Assume good faith, and give feedback that helps someone take their next step.
  2. Share work in progress. Unfinished is welcome. Early screenshots and half-built ideas are how things get better.
  3. Give credit. Name the people and projects that helped or inspired you.
  4. No spam. No mass pings, repeated posts or unrelated self-promotion.
  5. Keep secrets out. Never post API keys, tokens, settings files or private paths, in chat, issues or screenshots.

What goes in showcase.json

The file is a list. Add one entry for your project, keep the commas between entries, and put any screenshot in assets/showcase/ in the same pull request.

[
  {
    "title": "Your project's name",
    "by": "Your name or handle",
    "url": "https://example.com/your-project",
    "description": "One or two sentences about what it does.",
    "image": "assets/showcase/your-project.webp",
    "alt": "What the screenshot shows",
    "tags": ["game", "tool"],
    "added": "2026-10-01"
  }
]
FieldNeededWhat to put
titleYesThe project's name, up to 60 characters.
byYesYour name or handle, as you'd like it shown.
urlYesAn https:// link to try or see it: a site, a download, a repository or a video.
descriptionYesOne or two sentences, up to 200 characters.
imageNoA screenshot under assets/showcase/, 16:10 (1200×750), WebP, PNG or JPG, under 300 KB. Images on other sites are ignored.
altWith an imageWhat the screenshot shows, for people who can't see it.
tagsNoUp to 4 short words.
sourceNoAn https:// link to the code, when it differs from url.
thanksNoPeople who helped.
addedNoThe date, as YYYY-MM-DD. Newer entries show first.

Together in Studio

Your companion's Friends bubble is where people meet inside the app: rooms to talk in, music to share, companions that play together, and your own PCs kept in step. Each part below says where it stands.

The shared parts need the rooms hub. Rooms, Listen together, friends' playdates and cowork claims pass through the Void Engine rooms hub, which the owner runs on their own PC. Studio's side is built. They switch on once the hub is online with a public address, so they're marked Rolling out. Today's rooms are Void Engine Discord rooms, so they also use a linked Discord account.

Everything that stays on your own PC, like Practice with Pip, Your PCs and share files, works without the hub. Studios on the same Wi-Fi won't need it either, once Friends 2.0 lands.

What's in Friends

Rooms

New in 0.4.5Rolling out

Browse rooms, ask to join with a note, accept invites and chat with @names. Make a hangout room for up to 25 people or a cowork room for up to 10, open to requests or invite-only.

Links in room chat are never clickable, you can report a message, and Void Engine moderators can read every room.

Playground and playdates

New in 0.4.5Rolling out with friends

Pet your companion and pick its Personality. In the Playground, companions meet for short playdates: a high five, a race, rock-paper-scissors, hide and seek or a sticker swap. Practice with Pip works on your own PC with nothing sent.

Nothing about you is shared until you allow it, and What was sent lists every card that left your PC. When a friend's companion shares more with you, yours asks Share back? first.

Your PCs

In StudioNew in 0.4.5

See whether this PC matches your project on GitHub and what hasn't reached it yet. Sync this PC pulls and pushes, never force-pushes and never overwrites uncommitted work.

New in 0.4.5: Set up this PC checks what a new PC needs, Keep this PC up to date brings in your other PCs' pushes within about a minute, Sync runs the project's checks before it pushes, and each PC's line shows what its agents are working on.

Share files

New in 0.4.5

Share with friends saves one brain, recipe, team setup or set of model results as a .mefishare file. Studio strips paths and names, refuses secrets and shows you what's inside first. A friend's file is checked before it reaches your library and can never change your permissions.

Share between my PCs carries what you pick in a private GitHub vault, sealed with a key only your paired PCs hold.

Cowork claims

New in 0.4.5Rolling out

Link a cowork room to a project, and each builder claims the files it will edit before it starts. Every PC hears the claim within a second, so two PCs' agents never edit the same file at once. With no room or hub, nothing waits.

Link my Discord

Rolling out

Optional. It lets Rooms and Listen together know who you are. Studio reads only who you are and your roles in the Void Engine server, never your messages, your email or other servers.

It unlocks nothing: every theme and node style is free for everyone.

Reach your PCs from Discord

In progress

Check on your home PCs while you're away and talk to them in a direct message with the bot. Approvals sent from Discord need a PIN.

Studio's side is built and comes with 0.4.5, under Friends › Your PCs. It works once the bot's side, still being finished, is live.

Listen together

Rolling out

Play a link for one of your rooms. Friends press Listen along and hear it at the same point, and the room's Discord thread gets a note. Whoever put it on, or the room owner, can pause, restart or stop it. It takes YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud and Vimeo links, Discord files and plain audio links.

Share what I'm playing is off unless you turn it on. Then Void Engine members can see your current link or station with /nowplaying. It never shows a file name, and the hub forgets it when Studio closes.

In progress for 0.4.6

What we're building

This is the owner's plan for the community side of Studio: a place to share ideas and projects, and a reason to try each other's. None of it is built yet and the details are open, so now is the best time to say what you think.

  1. 1You share a project
  2. 2People play it or look at it
  3. 3They earn credits for that
  4. 4They spend credits to promote their own work, or on cosmetics

Share mixes, see what's played most

Planned

Share music mixes and see which ones get played the most, when you turn on share music taste in Friends or your profile. Today's opt-in Share what I'm playing is the first piece.

Ground rules, already decided

  • Credits are earned by taking part. You can't buy them.
  • Never for inviting people. Nothing earns credits for bringing people in, joining a server or posting lots of messages.
  • What's free stays free. Every theme and node style in Studio today is free for everyone. Cosmetics for credits would be new extras.
  • Music sharing is opt-in. Most-played lists count only people who turned on share music taste.
