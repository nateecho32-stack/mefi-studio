Hang out in the Void Engine Discord

Void Engine is Studio's community server. People share what they're building, swap model setups and keep each other company while they work. First-timers and unfinished projects are welcome, and you don't need Studio to join.

Join the Discord ↗

What people do there

Show what you're making

/showcase posts your project to #showcase with a short blurb and tags. You can thank someone who helped, and nobody gets pinged.

Cowork together

/cowork start with a topic opens a temporary voice channel and a thread. Both go away 5 minutes after the voice channel empties.

Get unstuck

Ask in a help thread. When a reply solves it, Mark as solution thanks the helper. /ask answers questions from the Studio guide.

Hear about new builds

Each new Studio release is posted once in the server, with a few highlights and the download link.

Studio, the community bot

The server's bot helps with sharing, coworking and questions. It runs on the owner's own PC, so it's online only while that PC runs it, and now and then it may be away.

In the Discord while the bot is online

  • /showcase Post a project with a title, a link and a description.
  • /cowork start A temporary voice channel and thread to work side by side.
  • /ask Answers from the Studio guide, up to 5 a day each.
  • Mark as solution The person who asked thanks whoever solved it.
  • /me Your points, streak and roles, shown only to you.
  • /privacy and /forget-me See what the bot keeps, or delete your records. It never stores message text.
  • Release posts One post per new Studio release.

Rolling out once the bot is linked to Studio’s relay

  • /room list and /room request Find rooms and ask to join from Discord. The room owner says yes.
  • /nowplaying What a member's Studio is playing, only if they turned sharing on.
  • Rooms themselves don't need the bot: from 0.5 you find and join them in Studio, under Friends › Rooms.

Roles for taking part

Roles come from showing up, sharing and helping, never from message counts or from inviting people. Regular takes four active weeks in a row. Builder, Helper and Mentor need a moderator's OK. Spotlight is the project of the week, for 7 days. Cowork Host, Room Host, Patron and Contributor are given by moderators.

/me shows your points and streak to you alone. There's no public leaderboard.

House rules

  1. Be kind. Assume good faith, and give feedback that helps someone take their next step.
  2. Share work in progress. Unfinished is welcome. Early screenshots and half-built ideas are how things get better.
  3. Give credit. Name the people and projects that helped or inspired you.
  4. No spam. No mass pings, repeated posts or unrelated self-promotion.
  5. Keep secrets out. Never post API keys, tokens, settings files or private paths, in chat, issues or screenshots.

What goes in showcase.json

The file is a list. Add one entry for your project, keep the commas between entries, and put any screenshot in assets/showcase/ in the same pull request.

[
  {
    "title": "Your project's name",
    "by": "Your name or handle",
    "url": "https://example.com/your-project",
    "description": "One or two sentences about what it does.",
    "image": "assets/showcase/your-project.webp",
    "alt": "What the screenshot shows",
    "tags": ["game", "tool"],
    "added": "2026-10-01"
  }
]
FieldNeededWhat to put
titleYesThe project's name, up to 60 characters.
byYesYour name or handle, as you'd like it shown.
urlYesAn https:// link to try or see it: a site, a download, a repository or a video.
descriptionYesOne or two sentences, up to 200 characters.
imageNoA screenshot under assets/showcase/, 16:10 (1200×750), WebP, PNG or JPG, under 300 KB. Images on other sites are ignored.
altWith an imageWhat the screenshot shows, for people who can't see it.
tagsNoUp to 4 short words.
sourceNoAn https:// link to the code, when it differs from url.
thanksNoPeople who helped.
addedNoThe date, as YYYY-MM-DD. Newer entries show first.

Together in Studio

Friends is where people meet inside the app: rooms to talk in, music to share, projects to play, companions that play together, and your own PCs kept in step. In 0.4.4 it's your companion's Friends bubble, where Your PCs works today. From 0.5 it's a place of its own on Studio's rail, opening on The Lobby. Each part below says where it stands.

Nothing to set up in 0.5. Rooms, room chat, Listen together, friends' playdates and cowork claims run through the Mefi Studio relay, a free service built into Studio, so no PC has to stay on. Sign in with Discord once (your account needs to be in the Void Engine server), and Studio connects by itself from then on. The relay keeps no chat: each Studio keeps its own copy of a room's messages for a week.

Everything that stays on your own PC, like Practice with Pip, syncing Your PCs through GitHub and share files, works without signing in.

What's in Friends

Rooms

Coming in 0.5

Start on The Lobby: who is online, the rooms open now and what was shared this week. Browse rooms, ask to join with a note, accept invites, or type a friend's invite code to come straight in. An open room reads like a chat app, with @names. Make a hangout room for up to 25 people or a cowork room for up to 10.

Links in room chat are never clickable and you can report a message. The relay keeps no chat, and moderators see a message only when someone reports it.

Playground and playdates

Coming in 0.5

Pet your companion and pick its Personality. In the Playground, companions meet for short playdates: a high five, a race, rock-paper-scissors, hide and seek or a sticker swap. Practice with Pip works on your own PC with nothing sent.

Nothing about you is shared until you allow it, and What was sent lists every card that left your PC. When a friend's companion shares more with you, yours asks Share back? first.

Your PCs

In 0.4.4More in 0.5

See whether this PC matches your project on GitHub and what hasn't reached it yet. Sync this PC pulls and pushes, never force-pushes and never overwrites uncommitted work.

Coming in 0.5: Set up this PC checks what a new PC needs, Keep this PC up to date brings in your other PCs' pushes within about a minute, Sync runs the project's checks before it pushes, and your PCs work as one: each PC you sign in on shows live, Send work here starts a task on another, and a busy PC or a laptop low on battery hands its ready work to a paired one.

Share files

Coming in 0.5

Share with friends saves one brain, recipe, team setup or set of model results as a .mefishare file. Studio strips paths and names, refuses secrets and shows you what's inside first. A friend's file is checked before it reaches your library and can never change your permissions.

Share between my PCs carries what you pick in a private GitHub vault, sealed with a key only your paired PCs hold.

Cowork claims

Coming in 0.5

Link a cowork room to a project, and each builder claims the files it will edit before it starts. Every PC hears the claim within a second, so two PCs' agents never edit the same file at once. Without a linked room, nothing waits.

Sign in with Discord

Coming in 0.5

Optional. It lets The Lobby, Rooms and the Project hub know who you are. Studio reads only who you are and your membership in the Void Engine server, never your messages, your email or other servers. No Discord roles are needed.

It unlocks nothing: every theme and node style is free for everyone.

Reach your PCs from Discord

Rolling out

Check on your home PCs while you're away and talk to them in a direct message with the bot. Approvals sent from Discord need a PIN.

Studio's side is built and comes with 0.5, under Friends › Your PCs, and the relay carries it. It works once the Studio bot is linked to the relay; the bot's side is still being built.

Listen together

Coming in 0.5

Play a link for one of your rooms. Friends press Listen along and hear it at the same point. Whoever put it on, or the room owner, can pause, restart or stop it. It takes YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud and Vimeo links, Discord files and plain audio links. Studio's side has been in the app since 0.4.0; from 0.5 it runs through the relay.

Rolling out Share what I'm playing is off unless you turn it on. Then Void Engine members can see your current link or station with /nowplaying, once the Studio bot is linked to the relay. It never shows a file name, and nothing about it is kept after Studio closes.

What we're building

This is the owner's plan for the community side of Studio: a place to share ideas and projects, and a reason to try each other's. Its first part is built for 0.5: the Project hub, credits and ranks, and a weekly Build Jam. Cosmetics, creator styles and shared mixes are still open, so now is the best time to say what you think.

  1. 1You share a project on the Project hub
  2. 2Someone plays it for two minutes
  3. 3You both earn credits
  4. 4Credits raise your rank and can feature your project for a day

How it works in 0.5. Spending credits on cosmetics is still a plan.

Share your projects

Coming in 0.5

Friends › Project hub lists what people make as cards: a public link, a title and a line about it, never a file. A star map draws every shared project, and New, Top and Mine list them. The Discord's /showcase and the wall on this page are still there too.

Earn credits by taking part

Coming in 0.5

Play someone else's project for two minutes and you both earn credits: 5 for the maker and 2 for you. A star earns the maker 3. Ranks run Spark, Ember, Flame, Comet, Star, Nova and Void, and others see your rank, not your balance.

Put your project in front of people

Coming in 0.5

Spend 100 credits you earned to put a project at the top of the Project hub for a day, once a week.

A weekly Build Jam

Coming in 0.5

Friends › Events opens a Build Jam every Monday with a theme: enter a shared project, play the others and vote. Co-work hours three times a day, and building together with a friend, earn credits too.

Cosmetics and creator styles

Planned

Use credits on new cosmetics, or offer styles you made for other people to use.

Share mixes, see what's played most

Planned

Share music mixes and see which ones get played the most, when you turn on share music taste in Friends or your profile. In 0.5 a playlist can already be shared into a room or onto the Project hub, and the opt-in Share what I'm playing is another first piece.

Ground rules, already decided

  • Credits are earned by taking part. You can't buy them.
  • Never for inviting people. Nothing earns credits for bringing people in, joining a server or posting lots of messages.
  • What's free stays free. Every theme and node style in Studio today is free for everyone. Cosmetics for credits would be new extras.
  • Music sharing is opt-in. Most-played lists count only people who turned on share music taste.
