**Mefi's Studio AI+ 0.5.0 is out!**

- **A new Studio:** two modes, Social (calm: Today, one box, what needs you, your friends) and Studio (sessions, the Map, Team and every tool). Ctrl+M switches.
- **Starting out is easier:** Start a new app, pick the AI that builds for you, and tell Studio what to make first, in plain words. No subscription? OpenCode's free models work.
- **Agents that handle themselves:** a task that can fix itself just does, and everything that needs you waits in one Inbox.
- **Friends built in:** rooms, chat, Listen together, the Lobby, Events and Your PCs, with no setup.
- **Make it yours:** twelve themes, interface scale from 70% to 150%, and updates you can roll back.
- **On 0.4.4?** It cannot finish an update by itself, so this one update is by hand: five short steps in the [release notes](https://github.com/nateecho32-stack/mefi-studio/releases/tag/v0.5.0). From 0.5 on, updates install in the app.
