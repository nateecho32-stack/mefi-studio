Mefi Studio is a free Windows app for building software with AI coding agents, with a community for sharing what you make. Join us in the [Void Engine Discord](https://discord.gg/xgfKc5pVxG) to meet other makers, or just hang out.

Version 0.5.0 is a new Studio: one calm layout with two modes, **Social** and **Studio**, a first run that explains itself, Friends that work with no setup, and the start of the move to Rust. [Explore Studio](https://nateecho32-stack.github.io/mefi-studio/) or follow the [setup guide](https://nateecho32-stack.github.io/mefi-studio/wiki/#/getting-started).

### Updating from 0.4.4: do it by hand, once

0.4.4 downloads updates but never finishes installing them, so move to 0.5.0 by hand this one time. From 0.5.0 on, updates install themselves (and can be rolled back).

1. Download `Mefi-Studio-AI+-v0.5.0-win32-x64.zip` from the [download page](https://nateecho32-stack.github.io/mefi-studio/download.html) or [GitHub releases](https://github.com/nateecho32-stack/mefi-studio/releases).
2. Quit Studio: right-click its tray icon and choose **Quit** (closing the window only hides it).
3. Extract the zip into a **new** folder, not over the old one.
4. Copy everything in the old folder's `resources\app\data` into the new folder's `resources\app\data`, except `curated.json` and `models.json` (keep the new copies of those two).
5. Open `Mefi Studio AI+.exe` in the new folder. Your settings, keys, projects and Discord link carry over by themselves.

Keep the old folder until you have checked that everything is there.

### A new Studio

- **Social and Studio.** Social is the calm mode: Today, one box to describe what you want (**Talk it over** or **Build it**), what needs you, and your friends. Studio is the in-depth mode: sessions, the Map, Team and every tool. Switch with the top bar or Ctrl+M; tabs, Search (Ctrl+K) and one Inbox (Ctrl+J) are shared.
- **Today, the Map and Team.** Today shows what needs you, what is running and what is done. The Map shows your agents at work, live. Team holds who builds, which AI each job uses (Providers, Seats and models), what they may decide (Permissions), and their Rules, Skills and Connectors.
- **Make it yours.** Twelve themes (Chrome is new and the default) plus your own palette, light or dark; interface scale from 70% to 150% (Ctrl +, Ctrl -, Ctrl 0); text size, density and detail with a live preview; panels you drag, fold and reset.

### Starting out is easier

- **Start a new app** leads the launch screen: name it, say what it should do, and Studio makes the folder.
- **Pick the AI that builds for you.** The first run says which account each tool uses (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Google), signs you in, and puts Studio on it. No subscription? OpenCode's free models are one click away.
- **What should Studio make first?** Type it in plain words, or tap an example. Answers come in plain words too: Explain like I'm 5 is the default answer style.

### Friends, built in

- Rooms, room chat, Listen together, the Lobby, Events (the weekly Build Jam) and the Project hub with credits and ranks run through Studio's own relay: no setup, and nothing of your projects is shared unless you share it.
- **Your PCs** shows every PC you sign in to, and can send work between them.

### Under the hood

- Agents think harder only when a job needs it, Claude Code and Codex steps show live, and finished work shows its changed files with Accept and Revert.
- Notifications on Windows, Undo for deleted tasks and ideas, plan versions, Report a problem, What's new after an update, and a rollback if an update misbehaves.
- Studio is moving to Rust in stages; 0.5.0 is still the Electron build, and its updater already knows how to install the Rust one later.

Studio stores project records and settings on your PC. Connected AI providers and coding tools may receive prompts and project context according to your configuration. See the [changelog](https://github.com/nateecho32-stack/mefi-studio/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for the full list.
