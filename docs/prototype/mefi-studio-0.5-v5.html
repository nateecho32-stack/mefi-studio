Mefi Studio 0.5

Revision 5 of the clickable prototype of the 0.5 shell, with the planned upgrades in place, on sample data for a fictional Notes app. Nothing here talks to a real project.

At this width the window below behaves like the app in a small window: the session list and the inspector open as drawers, and the tabs sit in one menu.

Try itThese buttons drive the sample data. Nothing is saved.

What changed in revision 5From the owner’s review of revision 4

  1. 1Two modes, one menu. Vibe is a calm board to watch, Build is list, thread and inspector; switch in the top bar or with Ctrl M.
  2. 2Tabs you add and pin. Studio manages them: one italic preview tab, tabs for agents that need you, idle and overflow closing, Recently closed.
  3. 3Resizable panels. Drag the edge of the list or the inspector, use the arrow keys, double-click to reset; each mode keeps its own sizes.
  4. 4Size and density with a live preview. Interface scale, text size, density and detail change a miniature beside the controls.
  5. 5Worktrees. One page shows every worktree, what is only on this PC, and merges or removes with a second press.
  6. 6Media stays visible. Image thumbnails with a lightbox, a before and after slider, a video card, device previews and a docked player.
  7. 7No scrollbars. Panes that can scroll show a thin line on their edge while you point at them or scroll, and it takes no space.
  8. 8Small windows. Under about 900 px the list and inspector become drawers and the tabs fold into one menu, down to 600 by 560. Under 620 px high the video card waits in the status bar.
  9. 9Same look, easier to reach. Same colours and parts, no text under 12 px, visible focus, keyboard for tabs, splitters and menus, calm motion when asked.

How each screen connects to the engine

The shell reads everything through one client store, fed by the existing pushes plus the new alerts:open. The host-side work below is shell-agnostic, so it can start before the shell decisions are made. Wave A is small and mostly pure modules, wave B is the agent and review core, wave C is breadth.
