What Studio is

A studio for making things, with company.

Describe what you want and agents build it in your project. You stay in charge, and people are close by when you want them.

Build with agents

Type an idea, a fix or a question. Build it puts coding agents to work in your project folder, and finished work waits in Review until its checks pass.

Rolling out

Friends in Studio

Friends › Rooms brings the community inside Studio: hangout rooms for up to 25 people and cowork rooms for up to 10, each with a plain-text chat. Listen together plays one link for everyone in a room, at the same point.

Both are built: Listen together is in 0.4.4 and Rooms come with 0.4.5. They switch on once the community's rooms hub is online.

Where we're headed

A place to share your ideas and projects. Earn credits by playing or looking at other people's projects, and spend them to promote your own, on cosmetics, or on styles other creators offer. Share mixes too, with a most-played list from people who turn on sharing their music taste.

Get started

Up and running in three steps.

Studio is a portable app: there is no installer and nothing else to set up first.

Download page →

  1. Download the zip

    Get the Windows zip from the latest release.

  2. Unzip and run

    Right-click the zip, choose Extract All, keep the folder together and open Mefi Studio AI+.exe.

  3. Get past SmartScreen

    The build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may warn you the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Stays on your PCTasks, conversations, plans and settings stay on your computer.
No Studio accountNo sign-up and no usage telemetry. Discord and AI connections are your choice.
Keys stay lockedAPI keys are encrypted with the Windows keystore, and Studio only shows "saved" or "not saved".
Your AI, your choiceUse a CLI login, an API key or a local model. Only the providers you connect get your prompts and project context.

Questions

Good to know

More answers in the wiki's FAQ, or ask in the Discord.

Is Mefi Studio free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, and every theme and node style is free. The AI services you connect may charge for their own use, so you can also use a subscription you already have or a local model.

Can I join before I have something to show?

Please do. Bring an unfinished idea, ask a question, give someone feedback or just hang out. You don't need to download Studio to take part.

Do I need Discord to use Studio?

No. Discord is the easiest way to meet the community, but Studio works without it and has no account of its own. Linking your Discord inside Studio is optional. Today Rooms use that link to know who you are, and ways for friends to connect without Discord are planned.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11. To build, connect a coding agent: Start here can install Codex, Claude Code, Grok or Antigravity and sign you in. A Linux build is planned.

Will agents change my files without asking?

Agents run real commands in the project folder you choose, so you decide how much they do alone. Always ask makes every new task wait for your OK, and Auto, the default, lets routine work run. Every automatic choice comes with a Why and an Undo.

What do the labels mean?
