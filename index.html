How it works

From one sentence to checked work.

You describe what you want. Studio plans it, builds it and checks it, and shows you every step along the way.

  1. 01

    Say it

    Type an idea, a fix or a question. Talk it over to think it through with Mefi, or press Build it to put it on the board. Drop files in for context.

  2. 02

    Mefi sizes it

    A small change goes straight to a builder. A bigger one is split into two to six steps. Each step leaves your project working, and your original request runs last to tie them together.

  3. 03

    Agents build it

    Coding agents pick up the work in your project folder. Each one shows up in the tree, flies to its task and tells you what it's doing.

  4. 04

    It gets checked

    Finished work waits in Review with its evidence until the checks pass or you confirm it. Here, "done" means verified.

Vibe mode

One calm screen for everything that matters.

Vibe puts the conversation, new work, live progress and your decisions in one place. Cards appear only when they have something to tell you, so a quiet project stays quiet.

  • Needs you: questions, approvals and stuck tasks, each answered in place
  • Building now: what every agent is doing, with live progress
  • Freshly done and Fresh ideas: what finished, and what's worth building next
  • New app: name it, describe it, and Studio makes the folder, starts git and begins building

Want every control? Switch to Build for the full workspace. Studio remembers which mode you like.

Command view

Watch your agents work, live.

Every session, task and agent is a node in a living tree. Builders orbit your assistant, fly to the task they're working on and report back in speech bubbles. The side panel keeps score: what's ready, what's verifying, what's waiting on you.

  • Several builders at once, each on its own task
  • Progress from the agent's own step list, not guesswork
  • Click any node for its details, history and evidence
  • Park the mouse at the edge for a slow Zen tour of the tree

While you wait

Put something on while they code.

Agents take a few minutes. Studio has a media player built in, so you can watch, listen or browse without leaving the app, and it tells you when the work is done.

YouTube and more

Play YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Spotify, Discord attachments or plain video and audio links in a floating player that stays put as you move around.

Up next

Search YouTube in the built-in explorer and build a queue. Next video plays what you queued first, and videos advance on their own.

Behind the tree

Make any video your background. Set its brightness and the tree's transparency, and let the tree drift toward the darker parts of the frame.

Fade on finish

When a task finishes, the video fades and Studio tells you. One click brings it back.

Browse inside the player

Browse here opens websites in the same player, with an address bar, back and forward. Copy a link anywhere and Studio offers to play or queue it.

Music moves the tree

Play your own music or ad-free radio. Turn on the audio link and the tree speeds up, sways and pulses with the beat.

Friends & linking

You stay in charge

Agents ask. You decide in one click.

When an agent hits a real choice, it asks you, explains why and suggests an answer. Pick an option, or type your own words, and the work carries on.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. Mefi's Studio AI+ is open source under the MIT license. You pay only for the AI you connect, and a local model or a subscription you already have works too.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11. Download the portable app, extract the folder and open Mefi Studio AI+.exe. No installer or Node.js needed. To build, you need a coding agent (Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, Grok or Antigravity). Setup can install and sign in to one for you.

Will agents change my files without asking?

Agents run real commands in the project folder you pick. You choose how much they do alone: Always ask approves each step, and Auto lets routine work run. Nothing starts until you open a project and turn the agents on.

Does my code leave my computer?

Only to the AI providers and coding tools you connect, when they work on a task. Studio itself has no servers, no account and no telemetry.

Can I watch YouTube in it?

Yes. The built-in player handles YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Spotify and plain media links. It floats over your work, can become the background, and fades when a task finishes.

Mac or Linux?

Studio is built and tested on Windows. It's open source, and other platforms are untested.

What will you build first?

Download Studio, open a project folder and describe your first idea.
