How it goes

From “what if” to “it works.”

  1. Hang outFriends chat in a room, with a song playing for everyone.
  2. Say what you wantType the idea into Social's box and press Send. Mefi turns it into tasks for your builders.
  3. Agents build itClaude Code, Codex or OpenCode work in your project while you keep chatting.
  4. You make the callsWhen a choice matters, it waits in your Inbox with a recommended answer.
  5. Checked, then yoursFinished work waits for its checks. Accept it, or take it back.
  6. Share itPut it on the Project hub. When friends play it, you both earn credits.

Why together

Vibe coding works until it doesn't. Then you want your people.

An AI can write the code. It can't sit next to you when something breaks, or tell you the idea is a good one. Studio puts the room back.

Ask the room

Stuck on a bug the AI keeps missing? Say it in a room. Someone is usually around, and co-work hours run three times a day.

Read it before it lands

Every change waits for you with the files it touched and the checks it ran. Accept it, revert a file, or talk it over with the room first.

Build next to someone

Cowork rooms hold up to ten. Work on your own thing or the same project: builders on each PC claim the files they edit, so they don't clash.

Show it the moment it works

Put it on the Project hub and friends play it in a click, then keep each other going with a Build Jam every week.

Questions

Good to know

Who is Mefi Studio for?

People who build things with AI and would rather not do it alone: friends making games and tools together, small teams, students, and communities that already hang out online. If you vibe code by yourself today, Studio gives you a room to do it in.

Does it replace Claude Code or Codex?

No. Studio has no AI of its own. It runs the coding tools you already use (OpenCode, Claude Code, Codex, Grok and Antigravity), an API key such as OpenRouter or z.ai, or a local model, with the login or key you already have. What it adds is the room around them: friends, chat, a board to steer every build, and a review of every change before it lands.

Is there voice chat or direct messages?

Not in 0.5. Rooms are text chat with @mentions and a shared player for music, and a room can be invite-only for just the two of you.

Is Mefi Studio free?

Yes. Studio is free and open source under the MIT licence. The AI tools you connect use their own plans or keys, and local models and OpenCode's free models cost nothing.

Do I need Discord?

Not to build. Friends (The Lobby, rooms, Listen together and the Build Jam) signs in with Discord through the Void Engine community server. Everything else works without it, on any Windows 10 or 11 PC, and Studio's first run helps you set up a coding tool or start free with OpenCode.

Will agents change my files without asking?

You decide. Permission modes go from Always ask to Auto, questions wait in your Inbox, and every change can be accepted or reverted, a file at a time or the whole attempt.

Windows warned me about the download. Is it safe?

The build isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Every release comes with a SHA-256 file so you can check the zip, and the source is on GitHub.

More in the wiki's FAQ, or ask in the Discord.
