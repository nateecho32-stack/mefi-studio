Read the showreel overview

An idea becomes a task. Agents appear in the live tree as work moves through queued, running, verifying and done. Eight node styles and eleven themes let you shape the workspace. Studio uses check, test and audit gates for its own releases, stores project records locally and has no usage telemetry. Your chosen AI providers receive requests. No Studio account is required.

WHAT'S NEW IN 0.4.2

A clearer way to build.

01 / VIBE

One focused workspace

Talk through ideas, create tasks and see what needs you. Answer questions, approve builds and retry blocked work directly from Vibe.

02 / VISUALS

Eight distinct node styles

Remastered shapes, motion and activity effects make the work easier to follow. Five styles are included; three belong to the optional Void collection.

03 / PLANNING

Keep your progress

Archive and restore plans, continue after a failed reply, and retain approvals when you rename a plan or save an unchanged specification.

04 / DECISIONS

Know what needs you

Clearer reasons for blocked work, fewer repeated offers and more useful task counts help you decide what happens next.

05 / EFFICIENCY

Less work while idle

Rendering and background checks avoid repeating unchanged work. Builders receive focused task context instead of the entire board.

06 / CONTROL

Your projects. Your AI.

Choose a provider, coding CLI or local model. Project records and settings stay on your machine; connected AI tools may receive prompts and project context.
